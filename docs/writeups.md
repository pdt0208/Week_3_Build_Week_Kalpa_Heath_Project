## Group summary

The project addresses two questions in the Kalpa Health data. Brief A asks why booked tests grew more slowly than the H1 plan. The notebook counts 435,008 booked tests in H1 2025 and 456,788 in H1 2026, a 5.01% increase against an 18% plan. Its volume decomposition attributes the change to unique patients (+3.76%), visits per patient (−0.97%), and tests per visit (+2.19%). The analysis also compares city contributions to the plan shortfall and reports that clinic bookings declined 11.9% while home bookings rose 37.5%.

Brief B asks whether Q2 booking declines in Chennai and Jaipur are real or affected by the Legacy-to-Nova transition. After excluding superseded rescheduled records consistently, the notebook reports bookings down 7.6% in Chennai and 10.7% in Jaipur, while the four Legacy cities combined grew 2.1%. Patient counts also fell in the two cities; the available analysis does not identify why. The appropriate next step is to reconcile the booking definitions with the city teams and investigate patient volume using acquisition, retention, access, or operational data before proposing a cause. These are descriptive findings, not proof that the system change or any specific operational factor caused the decline.
